⚡ ARCHITECTURE DOCTRINE

Engineered for speed & safety.

A bespoke Rust CLI designed from the ground up to replace clunky legacy bash pipelines.

⚡

Direct Kernel Procfs Reader

Bypasses sluggish process table scanners. Directly parses Linux /proc/net & macOS sockets for sub-millisecond discoveries.

🧠

Framework & Project CWD

Inspects process working directory and manifests to identify whether port 3000 is Next.js, Vite, NestJS, Postgres, or Redis.

🩺

Instant HTTP & Latency Prober

Test any socket with kport probe 3000 or press p in TUI to inspect headers, response latency & title.

🎯

Ranges, Wildcards & Presets

Slay entire microservice fleets with 3000..3010, prefixes with 80*, or presets :dev and :db.

🧟

Zombie Orphan Sweeper

Sweep and terminate abandoned background daemons whose parent terminals or IDEs died (PPID == 1) with kport --zombies.

🛡️

Safe Graceful Termination

Attempts clean SIGTERM first with timeout before escalating. Prevents accidental termination of vital OS daemons (sshd, systemd).
